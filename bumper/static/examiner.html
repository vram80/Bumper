How these numbers are computed

Window. Unless noted, figures use applications disposed of (allowed or abandoned) in the three years before the report date.

Allowance rate = allowed ÷ (allowed + abandoned). Abandonments before examination are excluded; abandonments for an unpaid issue fee count as allowances.

Actions per allowance / abandonment: non-final rejections, final rejections, restrictions, Ex parte Quayle actions and examiner's answers, averaged over allowed (or abandoned) applications.

Months to first action: filing or 371(c) date to the first non-final rejection, final rejection or allowance. Months to first allowance: filing or 371(c) date to the first notice of allowance.

Rank is the examiner's percentile for the raw value among peers with at least five dispositions (50% = median). Bars grow from the median: red leans toward longer or harder prosecution, green toward shorter or easier; in the experience rows gold means more experienced and blue less.

After-final, AFCP, pre-appeal and appeal-brief outcomes follow each filing to the next decisive event in the file history. Interviews report allowance rates among disposed applications that had at least one interview of each type.
